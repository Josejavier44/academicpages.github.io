Welcome to my personal webpage! I am a PhD student in Cosmology at the **Instituto de Física Teórica (IFT-UAM/CSIC)** in Madrid, working under the supervision of Yashar Akrami. 

My research is part of the **COMPACT Collaboration**, where I investigate the topology of the Universe from a fundamental physics perspective, applying Quantum Gravity and Quantum Field Theory to determine if our Universe might possess a non-trivial topology.

Before joining the IFT, I completed my Master's Degree in Theoretical Physics at the Autonomous University of Madrid, and my Bachelor's Degree in Physics at the University of Granada. I also have research experience studying quasi-normal modes of extremely compact objects (neutron stars) during an Erasmus+ stay at the **Niels Bohr Institute** in Copenhagen.

## Research Interests

My current work explores fundamental questions related to the shape and global structure of the cosmos. Specifically, my interests lie in:

* **Topology of the Universe:** Searching for signatures of non-trivial cosmic topology and their observational consequences.
* **Early Universe Cosmology:** Understanding the physical mechanisms that drove the universe's initial stages.
* **Inflation:** Exploring models of cosmic inflation and their predictions.
* **Dark Energy:** Investigating the nature of the accelerated expansion of the universe (such as Quintessence models).

## Beyond Physics

I am also a passionate musician holding a Professional Qualification in Music Performance (Oboe), and I enjoy photography. I am actively involved in science outreach and student representation within the Royal Spanish Physics Society.
